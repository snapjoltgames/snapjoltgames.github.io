Chaos Duo · Secret Rule Battles

Two players. One phone. Secret rules.

Sit face to face, lay the phone flat between you and battle in quick 30–60 second rounds. Each of you gets a secret rule. Follow it faster than your rival, then guess their rule for bonus points, or bluff them into a wrong guess.

Five chaotic mini-games

  • Secret Tap: tap only the shapes your rule allows.
  • Color Trap: hit your colour, dodge your trap.
  • Fake Button: one of four buttons is real.
  • Reverse Rules: when the warning sounds, everything flips.
  • Final Chaos: reverses and bombs, all at once.

Party Mode

  • Play 3, 5 or 7 games in a row and crown the champion.
  • Close finish? A tiebreaker game decides.
  • Easy, Normal or Hard, plus a head start for one player.

Made for playing together

  • Each half of the screen faces its player.
  • Your taps only count on your side.
  • Secret or public rules, your choice.

Friendly for everyone

  • Works offline. No account, no internet, no ads.
  • Colour letters option for colour-blind players.
  • Reduced motion, sound and vibration switches.
