Chaos Duo — Privacy Policy

App: Chaos Duo: 2 Player Party Game (Android, package com.snapjoltgames.chaosduo)

Developer: Snapjolt Games

Contact: snapjoltgames@gmail.com

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Summary

Chaos Duo is an offline party game for two players on one phone. It does not collect personal information, and it does not send or share any data with anyone.

What the game stores on your device

Chaos Duo saves two small files only on your device:

  • your settings: sound, vibration, reduced motion, colour letters, default rules, round time, level, head start, number of party games, the last mini-game you chose, and whether the tutorial has been shown; and
  • your style progress: how many matches have been finished, and which cosmetic styles are unlocked and selected.

The game engine (Godot) also keeps a graphics cache on the device so the game starts faster. It contains only compiled graphics code and no information about you.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The game turns off Android's app-data backup, so these files are not backed up to your Google account. (If you move to a new phone using Android's phone-to-phone transfer, Android may copy them to your new phone.)

Deleting your data: in the game, go to Settings → Privacy & Credits → Erase all my data to delete your settings and style progress. Uninstalling the game deletes everything it stored.

What the game does not do

  • No account or login.
  • No internet connection is used (the app does not request the Internet permission).
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking, no location, no camera, no microphone, no contacts.
  • No data is sold or shared with anyone.

Permissions

The app only requests permission to vibrate the phone for game feedback. Vibration can be turned off in Settings.

Children

Chaos Duo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects no personal information at all.

This website
